About

Badgertainment is a small indie studio that makes ready-to-use assets for farming, life-sim, cozy RPG and casual games.

Every pack sticks to one consistent cartoon style, so icons from different packs sit nicely together in the same inventory or shop screen. Packs come with clear file names, sprite sheets and data files, so you can drop them straight into your own item database.

About AI use: artwork and sounds are generated locally with open models (FLUX.2 Klein for images, Stable Audio for sounds). I then curate, clean up, resize, organize and quality-check everything by hand, and regenerate anything with defects. Every listing is marked as made with AI.

What you get in every icon pack

  • Transparent PNGs in 512, 256, 128 and 64 px
  • Silhouette versions for locked or undiscovered items
  • Sprite sheets with JSON and CSV rects
  • An items list with clear file prefixes
  • Commercial use allowed in your games
