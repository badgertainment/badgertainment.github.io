Cozy Fishing Icons

246 fishing icons: 113 fish species, sea creatures, rods, bait, loot, food and props.

246 colorful, cel-shaded fishing icons for cozy, farming and fishing games. Build a full fishing collection with inventory-ready icons in one consistent style with bold outlines.

What's inside

  • 113 fish: freshwater (trout, bass, pike, carp, catfish, sturgeon...), saltwater (tuna, cod, snapper, marlin, sharks, rays...), tropical (clownfish, tang, angelfish, lionfish, seahorse...), deep sea (viperfish, lanternfish, blobfish...) and legendary fish (crystal, lava, ghost, cosmic, frost, dragon...)
  • 17 sea creatures: crabs, lobster, octopus, squid, jellyfish, turtle, shells and more
  • 32 tools: 8 rods, reel, nets, traps, spear, harpoon, cooler, lantern, compass and gear
  • 21 bait & lures: worms, insects, crankbait, popper, jig, flies, spoons and more
  • 29 loot items: treasure chest, gems, crown, anchor, message in a bottle, junk and nature finds
  • 16 food items: sushi, fish & chips, grilled fish, soups, seafood plates
  • 18 props & extras: rowboat, dock, campfire, lighthouse, fish bowl, trophy, medal

Technical details

  • Transparent PNG in 512, 256, 128 and 64 px, each icon in its own file
  • Silhouette versions (512/256) for undiscovered slots in collection screens
  • Sprite sheets per size with JSON and CSV coordinates, plus items.csv
  • Clear prefixes: fish_, sea_, tool_, bait_, loot_, food_, prop_, misc_
  • Works in Godot, GameMaker, Unreal, web and any other engine

AI disclosure

The icon artwork was generated with FLUX.2 Klein 4B (Apache 2.0) running locally, then curated, cleaned up and quality-checked by hand. Defective icons were regenerated.
