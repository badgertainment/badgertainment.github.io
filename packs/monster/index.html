Monster Drops & Loot Icons

164 loot icons: monster parts, slimes, eggs, trophies, chests, coins, keys and relics.

164 colorful, cel-shaded item icons for RPG, dungeon crawler, roguelike and idle games. Every icon shares one consistent style with bold outlines, so your inventory, shop and crafting screens look like one set.

At a glance

  • 164 icons, transparent PNG
  • 4 sizes: 512, 256, 128 and 64 px
  • Dark silhouette versions (512/256) for locked or undiscovered items
  • Sprite sheets per size with JSON and CSV frame data
