Gems, Ores & Crafting Icons

199 crafting icons: ores, ingots, gems in 5 cuts, stone, wood, cloth, leather and essences.

199 colorful, cel-shaded item icons for RPG, crafting, survival and idle games. Every icon shares one consistent style with bold outlines, so your inventory, shop and crafting screens look like one set.

At a glance

  • 199 icons, transparent PNG
  • 4 sizes: 512, 256, 128 and 64 px
  • Dark silhouette versions (512/256) for locked or undiscovered items
  • Sprite sheets per size with JSON and CSV frame data
  • items.csv with file name, display name and category

What's inside

  • 18 ores: adamantite, cobalt, copper nugget, gold, iron nugget, obsidian, platinum and more
  • 26 ingots & bars: adamantite, bronze stack, copper, gold stack, mithril, obsidian stack, platinum and more
  • 57 gems: amber, aquamarine raw, citrine tear, emerald raw, onyx tear, ruby emerald, sapphire round and more
  • 18 stone & minerals: brick, clay, flint, geode, granite, lava rock, quartz and more
  • 15 wood: bamboo, beam, log birch, log magic, log pine, planks, sawdust and more
  • 16 cloth & thread: bandage, fabric blue, fabric red, needle, silk, string, thread red and more
  • 13 leather & hide: bone, dragon hide, feather, fur, fur white, hide, horn and more
  • 24 parts & components: anvil, bolt nut, chisel, hammer, lens, nails, saw and more
  • 12 essences & dust: air, arcane dust, crystal shard, earth, ether, fire, gold dust and more

Great for

  • Inventory, shop, crafting and collection screens
  • Quest rewards, loot drops and achievements
  • Godot, GameMaker, Unreal, RPG Maker, web, mobile and any other engine
