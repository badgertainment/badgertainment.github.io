Fantasy Weapons & Armor Icons

278 RPG icons in 12 tiers: swords, axes, bows, staffs, shields, armor, rings and amulets.

278 colorful, cel-shaded item icons for RPG, dungeon crawler, action and idle games. Every icon shares one consistent style with bold outlines, so your inventory, shop and crafting screens look like one set.

At a glance

  • 278 icons, transparent PNG
  • 4 sizes: 512, 256, 128 and 64 px
  • Dark silhouette versions (512/256) for locked or undiscovered items
  • Sprite sheets per size with JSON and CSV frame data
  • items.csv with file name, display name and category
