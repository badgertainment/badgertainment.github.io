PSX Dread: Retro Horror Props

113 low-poly horror props with a PS1-era look: clinic, haunted house, occult, basement, graveyard and more.

113 low-poly horror props, 335 ready-made variants, built the way games looked on the first PlayStation: low triangle counts, small point-filtered textures and that wobbly, grimy mood. Fill an abandoned clinic, a cursed house or a foggy graveyard in minutes.

What's inside

  • Clinic (15): hospital bed, gurney, wheelchair, IV stand, medicine cabinet, straitjacket, surgical lamp and tools
  • Haunted house (16): CRT TV, rotary phone, rocking chair, old radio, dolls and more
  • Lights (10): oil lanterns, candles, flickering bulbs and lamps
  • Occult (12): spirit board, ritual candles, skulls, altars and symbols
  • Blood, bone & butcher (10): meat hooks, cleavers, bones and stains
  • Inventory items (15): keys, notes, batteries, medkits and more, with inventory icons
  • Weapons (8): revolver, shotgun, fire axe, chainsaw, hammer, knife, crowbar, pipe
  • Basement (16) and Graveyard (11): boilers, shelves, crates, gravestones, crypt pieces

Built for retro horror

  • PS1-style low poly with small atlases (32 to 256 px), point filtering
  • Clean, dirty and bloody variants for most props
  • LODs and colliders on every model
  • Includes a retro vertex-snapping shader and standard materials
