Alchemy & Potions Icons

169 alchemy icons: potions in 10 colors and 5 shapes, elixirs, herbs, reagents and tools.

169 colorful, cel-shaded item icons for RPG, crafting, alchemy and cozy fantasy games. Every icon shares one consistent style with bold outlines, so your inventory, shop and crafting screens look like one set.

At a glance

  • 169 icons, transparent PNG
  • 4 sizes: 512, 256, 128 and 64 px
  • Dark silhouette versions (512/256) for locked or undiscovered items
  • Sprite sheets per size with JSON and CSV frame data
  • items.csv with file name, display name and category

What's inside

  • 50 potions: black big, blue small, cyan tall, green heart, orange square, teal big, white small and more
  • 21 elixirs & flasks: ampoule, bubbling, flask lab, jug, rainbow, star, triple and more
  • 26 herbs & plants: aloe, clover, frostleaf, lavender, mint, mushroom black, nightshade and more
  • 24 reagents: bat wing, bone, ectoplasm, fairy dust, firefly jar, honey, slime and more
  • 18 minerals & salts: amethyst, charcoal, glowstone, ice crystal, mercury, philosopher stone, ruby and more
  • 20 alchemy tools: alembic, candle, cauldron empty, funnel, hourglass, knife, mortar and more
  • 10 books & scrolls: journal, map, quill, recipe, rune stone, scroll sealed, spellbook and more

Great for

  • Inventory, shop, crafting and collection screens
  • Quest rewards, loot drops and achievements
  • Godot, GameMaker, Unreal, RPG Maker, web, mobile and any other engine
