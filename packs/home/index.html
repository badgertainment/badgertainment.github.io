Cozy Home & Decor Icons

176 home icons: furniture, beds, storage, lights, plants, decor, kitchen, bath and garden.

176 colorful, cel-shaded item icons for life-sim, cozy, house building and decorating games. Every icon shares one consistent style with bold outlines, so your inventory, shop and crafting screens look like one set.

At a glance

  • 176 icons, transparent PNG
