Cozy Food & Cooking Icons

208 food icons: fruit, vegetables, ingredients, baked goods, dishes, desserts and drinks.

208 colorful, cel-shaded item icons for cooking, restaurant, farming, survival and cozy life-sim games. Every icon shares one consistent style with bold outlines, so your inventory, shop and crafting screens look like one set.

At a glance

  • 208 icons, transparent PNG
  • 4 sizes: 512, 256, 128 and 64 px
  • Dark silhouette versions (512/256) for locked or undiscovered items
  • Sprite sheets per size with JSON and CSV frame data
  • items.csv with file name, display name and category

What's inside

  • 32 fruit: apricot, blackberry, dragonfruit, green apple, mango, papaya half, plum and more
  • 28 vegetables: asparagus, broccoli, celery, eggplant, lettuce, potato, spinach and more
  • 33 ingredients: bacon raw, chocolate bar, egg brown, honey jar, mustard, pepper grinder, rice bowl raw and more
  • 28 bakery: apple pie, bread loaf, cheesecake, cookie gingerbread, donut pink, muffin chocolate, scone and more
  • 35 meals & dishes: bento, fish chips, hotdog, nachos, pizza slice, ramen, spaghetti and more
  • 20 desserts & sweets: brownie, candy wrapped, chocolate truffles, cotton candy, fruit tart, honey cake, ice cream sundae and more
  • 20 drinks: apple cider, cocoa jar, coffee cup, iced coffee, lemonade, milk glass, orange juice and more
  • 12 snacks: cheese platter, chicken nuggets, chips bag, corn dog, crackers, dango, granola bar and more

Great for

  • Inventory, shop, crafting and collection screens
  • Quest rewards, loot drops and achievements
  • Godot, GameMaker, Unreal, RPG Maker, web, mobile and any other engine
