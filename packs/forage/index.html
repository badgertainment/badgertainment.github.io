Forest Foraging Icons

162 foraging icons: mushrooms, berries, herbs, flowers, nuts, leaves, critters and gear.

162 colorful, cel-shaded item icons for cozy, farming, survival and adventure games. Every icon shares one consistent style with bold outlines, so your inventory, shop and crafting screens look like one set.

At a glance

  • 162 icons, transparent PNG
  • 4 sizes: 512, 256, 128 and 64 px
  • Dark silhouette versions (512/256) for locked or undiscovered items
  • Sprite sheets per size with JSON and CSV frame data
  • items.csv with file name, display name and category

What's inside

  • 24 mushrooms: blue glow, chanterelle, crystal, fly agaric, golden, lions mane, parasol and more
  • 20 berries: berry basket, blueberry, cranberry, frostberry, gooseberry, huckleberry, lingonberry and more
  • 20 herbs & greens: burdock, chamomile, dandelion greens, four leaf clover, horsetail, mint, nettle and more
  • 20 wildflowers: bluebell, cornflower, dandelion, forget me not, heather, moonflower, poppy and more
  • 13 nuts & seeds: acorn, almond, beechnut, chestnut, golden acorn, hazelnut, maple seed and more
  • 15 leaves & branches: bark, branch, ginkgo, ivy, maple orange, oak, sapling and more
  • 18 bugs & critters: bee, bird egg, butterfly orange, cricket, feather blue, frog, moth and more
  • 15 forest finds: amber, arrowhead, bone, crystal, fossil, honeycomb, map scrap and more
  • 17 forager gear: backpack, bug net, canteen, gloves, journal, lantern, pouch and more

Great for

  • Inventory, shop, crafting and collection screens
  • Quest rewards, loot drops and achievements
  • Godot, GameMaker, Unreal, RPG Maker, web, mobile and any other engine

AI disclosure

The icon artwork was generated with FLUX.2 Klein 4B (Apache 2.0) running locally, then curated, cleaned up (background removal, trimming, centering, resizing) and quality-checked. Defective icons were regenerated.
