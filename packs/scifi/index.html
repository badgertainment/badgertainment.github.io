Sci-Fi Survival Icons

155 sci-fi icons: resources, tools, weapons, ammo, suit gear, tech, medical and cargo.

155 colorful, cel-shaded item icons for space survival, crafting, colony and shooter games. Every icon shares one consistent style with bold outlines, so your inventory, shop and crafting screens look like one set.

At a glance

  • 155 icons, transparent PNG
  • 4 sizes: 512, 256, 128 and 64 px
  • Dark silhouette versions (512/256) for locked or undiscovered items
  • Sprite sheets per size with JSON and CSV frame data
  • items.csv with file name, display name and category

What's inside

  • 21 resources: alien goo, biomass, copper ore, crystal red, ice, meteorite, stardust and more
  • 20 refined materials: battery, cable, chip, copper wire, glass panel, magnet coil, pipe and more
  • 20 tools: binoculars, drone, flashlight, hacking device, mining laser, pickaxe, repair kit and more
  • 16 weapons: emp grenade, grenade, mine, plasma gun, rifle, shield gen, smg and more
  • 8 ammo: battery ammo, bullets, darts, energy cell, magazine, plasma ammo, rockets and more
  • 16 suit & gear: armor, badge, boots, gas mask, goggles, jetpack, parachute and more
  • 22 tech & modules: ai core, beacon, cryo pod, gpu, keycard red, module speed, robot head and more
  • 10 medical: antidote, bandage, blood pack, medkit, nano heal, pills, radiation pills and more
  • 12 rations & food: algae, alien fruit, canned food, energy bar, hydroponic, meat alien, mushroom alien and more
  • 10 cargo & containers: barrel fuel, barrel toxic, canister, container, crate, crate small, loot box and more

Great for

  • Inventory, shop, crafting and collection screens
  • Quest rewards, loot drops and achievements
  • Godot, GameMaker, Unreal, RPG Maker, web, mobile and any other engine

AI disclosure

The icon artwork was generated with FLUX.2 Klein 4B (Apache 2.0) running locally, then curated, cleaned up (background removal, trimming, centering, resizing) and quality-checked. Defective icons were regenerated.
