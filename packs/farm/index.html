Cozy Farming & Harvest Icons

201 farming icons: crops, seeds, tools, animals, artisan goods, flowers and farm props.

201 colorful, cel-shaded item icons for farming, life-sim, cozy, crafting and survival games. Every icon shares one consistent style with bold outlines, so your inventory, shop and crafting screens look like one set.

At a glance

  • 201 icons, transparent PNG
  • 4 sizes: 512, 256, 128 and 64 px
  • Dark silhouette versions (512/256) for locked or undiscovered items
  • Sprite sheets per size with JSON and CSV frame data
  • items.csv with file name, display name and category

What's inside

  • 24 seed packets: beet, carrot, cucumber, lettuce, pepper, radish, strawberry and more
  • 45 crops & harvest: artichoke, carrot, cranberry, golden wheat, leek, pepper, starfruit and more
  • 20 tree fruit & saplings: acorn, apricot, cherry, coconut, mango, olive, peach and more
  • 28 farm tools: axe, fertilizer, hoe iron, pickaxe, scythe, sickle, watering can and more
  • 26 animals & animal products: chick, egg, egg duck, feed, hay, honeycomb, milk jug and more
  • 24 artisan goods: apple juice, candle, cloth, flour, maple syrup, pickles, rice sack and more
  • 14 flowers: bluebell, cosmos, daisy, lavender, orchid, rose, tulip red and more
  • 20 farm props: barn, cart, compost bin, fence, gate, mailbox, signpost and more

Great for

  • Inventory, shop, crafting and collection screens
  • Quest rewards, loot drops and achievements
  • Godot, GameMaker, Unreal, RPG Maker, web, mobile and any other engine
