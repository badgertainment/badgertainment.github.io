Cozy Isometric Furniture

251 isometric furniture pieces for 14 rooms, a building kit, 4 directions and 4 art styles. PNG sprites plus 3D models.

251 pieces of furniture and props, 14 rooms, 4 directions, 4 art styles, plus a building kit. Furnish homes, cafes, shops, gyms, libraries and gardens in your isometric game. Every piece can be rotated, because every piece comes from all four sides.

What's inside

  • 251 pieces in 14 rooms: Living Room (21), Bedroom (20), Kids Room (14), Kitchen (21), Dining (15), Bathroom (17), Office (17), Laundry (13), Library (14), Cafe (16), Shop (15), Gym (13), Game & Music (15), Garden & Patio (20), Decor & Pets (20)
  • Building kit (29 pieces): 9 floor tiles, walls with window, door and doorway in 7 finishes, 14 wall items (paintings, clock, mirror, TV, kitchen cabinet, shelves ...)
  • 4 directions for every piece: SE, SW, NW, NE
  • 4 art styles: Cozy Toon (outlined cel shading), Soft 3D, Flat Minimal and Pixel Art
  • Color variants: upholstered pieces in Cream, Sage, Rose, Navy, Mustard and Terracotta; wooden pieces in Oak, Walnut and White
  • 9,744 sprites at 256, 128 and 64 px tile width, plus a soft ground shadow for every piece and direction
  • Bonus 3D models: all 280 pieces as low-poly GLB and FBX

Made for isometric grids

  • True 2:1 isometric, 1 tile = 1 m. Footprints of 1x1, 2x1, 1x2 and 2x2 tiles listed for every piece.
  • The pivot of every sprite is the center of its footprint on the floor, so pieces snap onto your tiles.
  • furniture.json and items.csv list every sprite with style, variant, direction, footprint and pivot.
  • Pixel Art is drawn on a 64 px tile grid, 128 and 256 px are clean integer scales.

Engine package

Sprites with pivots already set, a FurnitureDefinition asset for every piece, the IsoFurniture component (style, color variant, direction, Rotate(), props on tables, footprint gizmo, automatic sorting), Tile assets for every floor and 14 furnished demo rooms. Works with any render pipeline.

The PNG files work in any engine: Godot, GameMaker, Unreal (Paper2D), Defold, web.
