Cozy Plants & Flowers Icons

260 plant icons: flowers, trees, houseplants, succulents, seed packets and 4-stage growth sets.

260 colorful, cel-shaded item icons for farming, gardening, life-sim and cozy games. Every icon shares one consistent style with bold outlines, so your inventory, shop and crafting screens look like one set.

At a glance

  • 260 icons, transparent PNG
  • 4 sizes: 512, 256, 128 and 64 px
  • Dark silhouette versions (512/256) for locked or undiscovered items
  • Sprite sheets per size with JSON and CSV frame data
  • items.csv with file name, display name and category

What's inside

  • 50 flowers: anemone, cornflower, foxglove, iris, magnolia, poppy, sunflower and more
  • 10 bouquets: flower crown, lavender, pastel, roses, sunflowers, tulips, vase daisies and more
  • 25 trees: apple, bush flower, cypress, hedge, magic tree, maple autumn, palm and more
  • 30 potted houseplants: african violet, begonia, calathea, herb basil, monstera, peace lily, snake plant and more
  • 20 cacti & succulents: agave, cactus barrel, cactus flower, cactus saguaro, cactus tall, haworthia, jade and more
  • 25 seed packets: bamboo, cosmos, herb mix, magic, orchid, pumpkin, sunflower and more
  • 80 growth stages (4 per plant): apple tree 1, blueberry 4, carrot 3, eggplant 2, pepper 1, pumpkin 4, sunflower 3 and more
  • 20 garden supplies: bee hotel, fertilizer, garden gloves, hanging pot, plant food, planter box, pot empty and more

Great for

  • Inventory, shop, crafting and collection screens
  • Quest rewards, loot drops and achievements
  • Godot, GameMaker, Unreal, RPG Maker, web, mobile and any other engine
