Cozy Cooking States Icons

90 ingredients in 4 states each: raw, chopped, cooked and burnt. Made for cooking and restaurant games.

360 cel-shaded cooking icons: 90 ingredients, each in 4 matching states (raw, chopped, cooked and burnt). Swap the icon as the timer runs and your cooking, restaurant or farming game shows progress at a glance.

At a glance

  • 90 ingredients x 4 states = 360 icons, transparent PNG
  • 4 sizes: 512, 256, 128 and 64 px, same file names in every size
  • Matching names: raw_tomato, chopped_tomato, cooked_tomato, burnt_tomato
  • State strips: one 4-frame strip per ingredient for progress-bar style swaps
  • Sprite sheets with one row per ingredient (raw, chopped, cooked, burnt) plus JSON and CSV rects
  • Dark silhouette versions (512/256) for locked recipes
  • items.csv with file name, display name and category

Great for

  • Cooking timers, recipe books and kitchen stations
  • Restaurant, farming and life-sim games
  • Godot, GameMaker, Unreal, web, mobile and any other engine

AI disclosure

The icon artwork was generated with FLUX.2 Klein 4B (Apache 2.0) running locally, then curated, cleaned up and quality-checked by hand. Defective icons were regenerated.
