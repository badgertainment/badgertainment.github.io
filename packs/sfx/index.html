Cozy Game SFX

212 soft sound effects in 10 folders: UI, items, farming, animals, footsteps, magic, ambience loops and jingles.

212 sound effects for cozy, farming, life-sim and casual games: UI clicks and chimes, item pickups, farming tools, farm and pet animals, footsteps, kitchen sounds, magic sparkles, nature, seamless ambience loops and short jingles.

At a glance

  • 212 sounds in 10 clearly named folders
  • WAV (44.1 kHz, 24-bit) and OGG Vorbis versions of every sound
  • One-shots trimmed and peak normalized to -1 dBFS, mono for easy positional audio
  • 15 seamless stereo ambience loops (about 13 s each), loudness matched
  • sounds.csv with file name, folder, duration and description

What's inside

  • 40 UI: click soft, click wood, click bubble, hover, pop, pop high and more, filtered for a soft, warm top end with no harsh highs
  • 30 items & inventory: pickup, pickup soft, drop, drop grass, equip, bag open and more
  • 30 farming & tools: hoe dig, shovel, water can, seed plant, harvest pluck, harvest crop and more
  • 25 animals: chicken, chick, rooster, cow, sheep, goat and more
  • 15 footsteps: grass, dirt, wood, stone, snow, sand and more
  • 20 cozy home & kitchen: kettle, boil, sizzle, chop, cup clink, pour tea and more
  • 15 magic & sparkle: sparkle, sparkle long, cast, heal, teleport, fairy and more
  • 10 nature & weather: thunder, rain start, wind gust, leaves rustle, stream, waterfall small and more
  • 15 ambience loops: forest day, meadow, night, rain roof, rain window, river and more
  • 12 jingles & stingers: success, level up, quest done, fail, new day, night and more
