NPC Emote Bubbles

450 animated emote bubbles for NPCs in 5 art styles: cozy, pixel, ink, flat and manga.

450 emote bubbles in 5 art styles for the little moments above your NPCs' heads: a quest marker, a heart, an angry puff, a sleepy Zzz. Every bubble comes in speech, thought and shout shapes, with ready-made animation frames and a drop-in C# component.

At a glance

  • 5 styles: Cozy Cartoon, Pixel Art, Ink Sketch, Flat Minimal, Manga
  • 3 bubble shapes per style: speech, thought, shout
  • 30 emotes: love, heartbreak, happy, laugh, sad, angry, shocked, sick, sleepy, dizzy, nervous, alert, question, silence, idea, music, excited, trade, gift, hungry, thirsty, quest available, quest complete, yes, no, danger, battle, waiting, cold, fired up
  • 450 bubble sprites + 150 loose emote icons + 15 empty bubbles
  • Transparent PNG in 512, 256, 128 and 64 px. Pixel Art is built on a true 64 px grid.
  • Animation sheets for every bubble: pop-in (6 frames), idle bounce (8 frames, loop), pop-out (5 frames), with JSON and CSV frame data
  • Same canvas and pivot (the tail tip) for every bubble of a style, so emotes swap without jumping
  • Sprite sheets, items.csv and bubbles.json (pivot + content area)
  • Engine package: EmoteBubble C# component (pop-in, bounce, pop-out, face camera), prefab, 15 ready sets and a demo scene

Great for

  • RPGs, farming and life-sim games, quest givers and shopkeepers
  • Villager reactions, stealth alerts, status and mood indicators
  • Godot, GameMaker, Unreal, RPG Maker, web, mobile and any other engine

The village background and NPC characters in the preview images are for the demo only and are not part of the asset set.

AI disclosure

The artwork was generated with FLUX.2 Klein 4B (Apache 2.0) running locally, then curated, cleaned up (background removal, pixel-grid snapping), composited into bubbles, animated and quality-checked. Defective images were regenerated.
